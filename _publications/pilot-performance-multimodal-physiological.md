This paper presents algorithms to estimate the cognitive stress of pilots landing in difficult conditions from physiological and behavioral signals (EMG, ECG, gaze tracking, etc.). Multimodal timeseries data was synchronized using dynamic loading, enabling 140 GB of data to be loaded and preprocessed in 5 minutes. We benchmarked different model architectures for scalability and accuracy, evaluating TSFresh, an ensemble of traditional time series characteristics, and MINIROCKET, a fast featurizer using random convolutional kernels.
